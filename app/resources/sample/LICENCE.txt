sample-10s.wav

Generated for InnyTypes, from nothing but a formula: a 10-second tone sweeping from 220 Hz to
880 Hz, 16 kHz mono 16-bit PCM. No recording, voice or third-party material is in it.

    ffmpeg -f lavfi -i "aevalsrc=0.25*sin(2*PI*(220*t+33*t*t)):s=16000:d=10" \
      -ac 1 -c:a pcm_s16le -map_metadata -1 -fflags +bitexact -flags:a +bitexact sample-10s.wav
